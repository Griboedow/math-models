# Vehicle models

| Calculator | Examples | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| Printed toy vehicle physics | Trike MINI v06, six-wheel support demo and independent floating bodies | [Calculator README](toy-physics/README.md) |

The calculator covers N-wheel support, surfaces, local axle strength and
independent buoyancy/contact/cleaning comparisons. PLA is the default material;
PETG and property overrides are available. Its numerical checks and scope are
documented alongside the equations and inputs.

Printable models remain in the [vehicle CAD catalog](https://github.com/Griboedow/3d-Models/tree/main/vehicles).
