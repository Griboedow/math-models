What the numbers mean

This is a mechanical model, not a health or hand-comfort assessment. The ball is an ellipsoid, the yarn is an elastic link, and bearings have static and running friction. Ball size stays fixed during each short run.

After ball slip or stand movement, the curve is only a hypothetical continuation with enforced coupling. The snag threshold is a static estimate, not an impact prediction. Slack and fiber snags are not fully modeled.

Knitting and crochet rhythms are editable examples. Friction and elasticity are unmeasured assumptions. The server runs only on your computer.
